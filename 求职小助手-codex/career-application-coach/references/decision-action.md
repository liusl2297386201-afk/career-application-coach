# 职业路径规划&近期行动目标

Use as section eight of a complete application case, or for a short next-step request. It is a compact career decision tool, not a substitute for a full long-term career plan.

## Direction card

State:

- `current decision`: the role, direction, or next evidence to validate;
- `primary path`: the job mechanism the user should build toward;
- `adjacent path`: a credible nearby direction and its different evidence requirement;
- `confirmed capability combination`: facts proven in at least one real project, with stronger confidence when repeated across projects;
- `key evidence gap`: the one missing proof most likely to change competitiveness;
- `revision condition`: the fact that would change the recommendation;
- `review point`: a date or observable event for reassessment.

Do not call a preference, title, or employer brand a confirmed long-term strength without behaviour or result evidence.

## 30/60/90-day action card

Give no more than three actions. Each must have:

`time window | deliverable | observable signal | what decision it updates`

Good deliverables include a JD-specific resume candidate, an evidence-backed portfolio case, an application set, an informational interview summary, or a public-data simulation labelled as such. Avoid generic actions such as “improve communication” or “learn more.”

Use the dedicated career-planning skill when the user asks for a full multi-year path, an offer decision, a career switch, or a persistent career record.
