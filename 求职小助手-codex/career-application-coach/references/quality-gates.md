# Quality Gates and Regression Checks

Use before delivering a complete application case, recruiter audit with rewrites, or upgraded case.

## Baseline completeness

- [ ] The role mechanism and application decision are explicit.
- [ ] Every hard and high-priority core requirement appears in the evidence matrix.
- [ ] The matrix distinguishes direct match, transferable match, evidence insufficient, and hard gap by work mechanism.
- [ ] Two or three core cases have a known-fact STAR and a case thesis.
- [ ] Resume advice states what must remain, then gives only evidence-backed candidate edits.
- [ ] The interview map has at least 12 questions across three categories.
- [ ] Portfolio cases identify available evidence, collection needs, and confidentiality boundaries.
- [ ] The direction card includes a primary path, adjacent path, evidence gap, action, observable signal, and review point.

## Evidence and rewrite integrity

- [ ] No missing material has been described as absent experience.
- [ ] No metric, title, date, platform, collaborator, delivery object, mechanism, or result has been invented.
- [ ] No module, metric, platform, collaborator, delivery object, or result disappeared merely to make prose shorter.
- [ ] STAR improves causal legibility without adding ownership, causality, budget, ROI, paid acquisition, or commercial responsibility.
- [ ] Candidate wording and inference are visibly different from confirmed facts.

## Dialogue integrity

- [ ] The baseline case appears before any question.
- [ ] There are no more than five evidence-upgrade questions in a turn.
- [ ] Every question names the exact resume, STAR, interview, portfolio, or action item it could change.
- [ ] Answered or irrelevant questions are not repeated.

## Optional PDF integrity

- [ ] After every complete v0/v1/v2 report, the user was asked: `是否需要我将该版内容整理为 PDF输出？（若不回答该问题则默认不需要）`.
- [ ] No HTML or PDF was created without explicit user request.
- [ ] No response to the PDF question was treated as not needing a PDF, without repeating the question for the same version.
- [ ] A requested PDF contains the complete current report, is content-first, and has no unnecessary visual design.
- [ ] A requested PDF was rendered and visually checked before delivery.

## China game-publishing regression checks

- [ ] Do not make a major-company internship the lead by prestige alone.
- [ ] Preserve separate content-distribution, community-operation, and livestream/event-operation modules when they prove distinct mechanisms.
- [ ] Do not force product name, genre, audience, attribution, or extra metrics into a resume body without a material reason.
- [ ] Do not promote creator support, feedback collection, or content coordination into budget ownership, paid acquisition, ROI, or campaign leadership without evidence.
